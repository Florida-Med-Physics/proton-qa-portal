GatorQA — Quick User Guide

1. Sign in and choose a QA list from Dashboard or Perform QA.
2. Check the QA date. Use the matching report found in your import folder, or choose a measurement file. Check the import summary; HDR PDFs require review before filling values.
3. Enter the remaining measurements or Pass/Fail answers. Next unanswered jumps to the next missing answer. Show unfinished only hides answered tests; switch it off to review all results.
4. Mark in Progress keeps your entries without submitting and takes you back to Perform QA; open the list again to carry on. Submit for review when ready. Unanswered tests are not passes. Reports download only when requested.
5. In Review QA, your queue comes first: complete the therapist review or the physicist approval for your role. After Approve, the next run waiting for you opens. Review notes are optional. If old Sphinx files were imported as waiting for review, a Physicist or Admin can use Record past imports as history… on Data Administration, under Import folders; their results and verdicts are kept.
6. Chambers and electrometers are kept in Setup QA, More, Equipment: add a device once and type its values there; Connect a QA List… makes a list choose its chamber from that page and fills the tests you tick from it. On the QA form you choose the device and its values are filled, not typed.
7. Use Machine Trends for measurements over time, one QA list at a time. In Setup QA, open Machines to edit machine limits, then Save template.

Import setup: In Setup QA, choose Add import block. Select a reader preset or map Excel cells, CSV/TXT columns, or report labels to tests. Preview a sample, choose Use mapping, then Save template. During Perform QA, review imported values before filling answers. PDF scans use OCR; check recognized values carefully.
8. On the QA form, Column Chooser (or a right-click on a column heading) opens a small window that shows the table's columns side by side, in their order, and hides or moves them: tick a column, use ← →, or drag a name left or right, onto a heading, or a heading into the window to hide it. A column's heading can also be dragged left or right. The window stays open while you work; move it by its title, close it with ✕. The choice is kept on that computer. The ⓘ beside a test or group opens its directions, the ⓘ beside a section's title lists that section's directions, and Protocol opens the list's protocol.
9. A step that is only an instruction: in Setup QA, set its Result Type and Save. "Direction: test below" shows it behind the ⓘ of the test (or group) under it; "Direction: section" lists it behind the ⓘ beside its section's title. Either way it has no row on the QA form and nothing is recorded.
10. The report a check comes from: in Setup QA, ✎ Edit the test and fill Reference. The QA form shows it behind the test's ⓘ. GatorQA does not check a reference against the report.
11. To add to a section in Setup QA, use ＋ Test or ＋ Block on the section's title: the new test or block goes to the end of that section and the list is saved.
12. On the QA form a test is one line of a grid of cells: its limits are in the Warning ± and Action ± columns (on a narrow window they show under its name instead), and the box you fill is the Measured cell. Tests that belong together share a line, each beside its own name. A table that a measurement file fills (several energies or spots) is closed: its line says from file, then PASS, WARNING or ACTION with the worst row named. Click the line, or Show, to see its rows; nothing opens by itself.
